-----------
Email templates use useLingui() hook which requires I18nProvider context.
Previous attempts to avoid I18nProvider failed because templates NEED it.

SOLUTION:
---------

- Wraps components in I18nProvider (so useLingui() works)
- Uses @react-email/render directly (no Tailwind/Branding complexity)
- Avoids Suspense and React version conflicts
